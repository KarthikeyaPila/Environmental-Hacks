# What is Phir?

## What problem does it solve?

Recoverable household material often gets mixed into ordinary waste before it
reaches the recovery chain. The existing network of households, kabadiwalas,
and recycling companies exists, but the handoffs between them are difficult to
see, coordinate, and track.

Phir is a digital coordination layer that gives recyclable material a clear
path:

**Household → Collection partner → Recycler**

## Who is it for?

- **Households:** record recyclable material, get optional photo-assisted
  identification, request collection, and track their contribution.
- **Collection partners:** find nearby recovery requests, accept and complete
  collections, and manage their inventory.
- **Recycling companies:** publish material requirements, view available
  recovered material, and book matching quantities.

## The video in one sentence

Phir helps more recyclable material move from a household doorstep into the
existing recovery network instead of being lost in the ordinary waste stream.
